# Wildlife audio credits

These are short, normalized derivatives prepared for Arctic Research Simulator.

- `beluga.mp3` — **Beluga vocalizations**. Source: NOAA National Marine Fisheries Service, via Wikimedia Commons `Beluga vocalizations.ogg`. Public domain (PD-US-NOAA). Original recording credited on Commons to Fisheries Research Agency, Japan, with contract support from NOAA to Hubbs-SeaWorld Research Institute.
- `humpback.mp3` — **Song of Humpback Whales**. Source: Wikimedia Commons `Humpbackwhale2.ogg`, author Spyrogumas. CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication.
- `walrus.mp3` — **Walruses Hauled Out on the Northwest Coast of Alaska**. Source/credit: Anthony Fischbach, U.S. Geological Survey Alaska Science Center. Public domain.
- `arctic-tern.mp3` — **Arctic Tern XC564600**. Recordist: Doug Hynes; source Xeno-canto, mirrored on Wikimedia Commons. Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. This trimmed/normalized derivative remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.
