Privacy Policy

Arctic Research Simulator

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Arctic Research Simulator is designed to collect as little personal information as practical. The iOS app does not require a user account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, health information, or physical location.

Information the app collects

Product interaction data. The app may send limited analytics about how the game is used, such as app or game launches, onboarding progress, missions, grants, vessel or equipment choices, publications, wildlife observations, broad in-game regions, and session duration.

Purchase analytics. For in-app purchases, the app may record limited analytics such as the product selected and whether the purchase succeeded, was cancelled, or failed. We do not send payment-card information or StoreKit transaction identifiers to our analytics provider.

Performance data. The app may send limited technical performance information, such as an approximate frame-rate sample and basic screen/device-class information, to help identify performance problems.

How this information is used

We use this information to understand how the game is used, improve gameplay and features, and diagnose or improve app performance. We do not use this information for third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or data-broker purposes.

Analytics provider

Arctic Research Simulator uses PostHog for product analytics. The iOS app is configured to minimize analytics collection: it does not use session replay or automatic interaction capture, does not create identified user profiles, and does not intentionally attach names, email addresses, Apple IDs, or other direct identity information to analytics events.

In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment-card or bank-account information. Apple’s handling of purchase and account information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies.

Location

The app’s Arctic map and geographic locations are part of the game world. The iOS app does not collect your device’s precise or coarse physical location for analytics.

Data linking and tracking

The analytics described above is not intended to be linked to your real-world identity, and it is not used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Arctic Research Simulator evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
