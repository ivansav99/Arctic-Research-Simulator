## App Store description

Take command of an Arctic research expedition.

Start with a small vessel in Svalbard and build a scientific career through fieldwork, grants, publications, and increasingly ambitious expeditions. Recruit scientists, equip your ship with real-world research systems, manage food and fuel, navigate sea ice and weather, and decide which opportunities are worth pursuing.

EXPLORE THE ARCTIC  
Sail between Arctic ports, fjords, research sites, sea-ice zones, and open-ocean stations on a polar chart shaped by seasonal conditions.

BUILD A RESEARCH PROGRAM  
Accept grants, assemble a team, buy instruments, complete field stations, collect data, publish papers, earn citations, and advance from graduate-level work to major research programs.

OUTFIT YOUR VESSEL  
Progress from small local boats to global research vessels and icebreakers. Add oceanographic, atmospheric, biological, acoustic, sea-ice, drone, helicopter, ROV, mooring, and coring capabilities.

DISCOVER WILDLIFE  
Encounter Arctic whales, seals, walrus, seabirds, and other wildlife. Record observations and build field-guide collections with photographs, facts, and authentic animal sounds.

MAKE EXPEDITION DECISIONS  
Manage budgets, crew, equipment, supplies, ship capability, weather, sea ice, mission deadlines, and scientific priorities. A successful expedition requires more than simply reaching the destination.

Arctic Research Simulator is a science-management and exploration game inspired by the real work of modern Arctic field research.

## Privacy policy draft

**Arctic Research Simulator Privacy Policy**

Last updated: [RELEASE DATE]

Arctic Research Simulator is designed to work without creating a user account.

### Information we collect

The app uses PostHog to collect limited, anonymous product analytics so we can understand how the game is used and improve gameplay. These analytics may include game launches, onboarding progress, ports visited, grants and missions, vessel and equipment choices, publications, wildlife interactions, broad in-game region, resource levels, and active play time.

The app may also record anonymous analytics about in-app purchase activity, such as which Private Funding package was selected and whether the purchase succeeded, failed, was cancelled, or remained pending.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, real-world GPS location, advertising identifier, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, health information, or payment-card details.

### In-app purchases

Private Funding purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment information. Arctic Research Simulator does not receive your credit-card or bank-account details.

### Analytics and tracking

Analytics are used only to understand product usage and improve the app. The app does not use analytics data for advertising, does not sell personal data, and does not use the data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Session replay and automatic click/page capture are disabled. The analytics configuration does not create persistent user profiles across app launches.

### Local game data

Saved games are stored locally on the device. They are not uploaded to an Arctic Research Simulator account because the app does not require or provide an account.

### Third-party services

Anonymous product analytics are processed using PostHog's U.S. cloud service. In-app purchases are processed by Apple.

### Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not include chat, social networking, or user-generated content sharing.

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be posted at the Privacy Policy URL listed on the App Store.

## App Review notes

Arctic Research Simulator is a single-player science-management and exploration game. No login or account is required.

To begin:
1. Tap NEW GAME.
2. Select a Chief Scientist avatar and scientific specialty.
3. The expedition begins in Longyearbyen, Svalbard.
4. Enter the port interface to recruit scientists, purchase equipment, resupply, and accept Research Grants.
5. Tap research targets on the chart to navigate to them and execute missions.

In-App Purchases:
- Enter any port.
- Tap APPLY FOR PRIVATE FUNDING in the port interface.
- Three consumable products are offered:
  - ars.private_funding.1m
  - ars.private_funding.10m
  - ars.private_funding.50m
- Each successful StoreKit transaction adds the displayed amount of in-game expedition cash.
- These purchases do not unlock separate content and may be purchased repeatedly.

No external payment mechanism is present.

The game contains privacy-minimized anonymous product analytics. It does not require account creation and does not use cross-app tracking.

Core game assets are bundled with the app. Higher-resolution Arctic terrain outside the bundled starting region may be downloaded and cached when network access is available.
